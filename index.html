⛪ Church Attire Required

As we enter God's house, we ask all guests to dress modestly and respectfully in honor of the sacred sacrament being celebrated.

  • Shoulders covered — Ladies, please wear tops with sleeves or bring a shawl or cardigan. Sleeveless and off-shoulder tops are not permitted inside the church.
  • Modest hemlines — Skirts and dresses should fall at or below the knee. Shorts and mini skirts are not appropriate for the church setting.
  • No sheer or revealing clothing — Avoid see-through fabrics or plunging necklines. Elegance and modesty go hand in hand.
  • Smart casual or formal for men — Collared shirts, slacks, or barong are ideal. Sleeveless undershirts and athletic wear are not allowed inside the church.
  • Comfortable footwear — Closed shoes or elegant sandals are recommended. The church floor may be cold or uneven in some areas.

Dress in shades of white and pink to honor this pure and tender milestone. Your presence, dressed in these gentle hues, will make this day even more beautiful.
